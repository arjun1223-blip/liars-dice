    Table talk

    How to play

    1. Setup: everyone gets five dice. Each player rolls one die. The highest roll makes the opening bid, and play continues to the left (clockwise).
    2. All players roll their dice in secret. Ye only see yer own.
    3. A bid is a guess about all the dice on the table, like "6 × 5s": there are at least six 5s in total.
    4. On yer turn ye must raise the bid or call "Liar!". There is no passing.
    5. Raising: increase the quantity, the number, or both. To increase the number, bid the same or a greater quantity (6 × 5s → 6 × 6s).
      • Standard mode: when ye increase the quantity, ye may bid a lower number (3 × 3s → 4 × 2s).
      • Hard mode: the number can never go down. Once someone bids 6s, only the quantity can rise.
    6. Ones are wild and count as any number. A player with three 4s and two 1s has five 4s. Nobody may open a round by bidding ones.
    7. Liar! Everyone reveals. Count the bid number plus all ones. If the total is equal to or greater than the bid, the challenger loses a die. If it's less, the bidder loses a die.
    8. The player who lost a die opens the next round. If they're out of dice, the player to their left opens.
    9. Palafico: while anyone is down to one die, every round is a palafico round. A one-die player opens it. If several players have one die, the opener passes clockwise from the last opener to the next one-die player. In a palafico round, ones may be bid to open and are not wild, and the number can't change from the opening bid. This applies even when only two players remain. Palafico ends when nobody has exactly one die.
    10. The last pirate with dice wins.
